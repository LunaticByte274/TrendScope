# TrendScope — User Guide & Demonstration Manual

## 1. Getting Started
TrendScope is accessible through any modern web browser. The user interface provides real-time exploration of YouTube trending patterns, comparative cross-country analytics, and predictive machine learning models.

---

## 2. Navigating the Interface

### 2.1 Global Controls (Top Navigation Bar)
- **Country Selector:** Toggle between all 10 supported territories: USA (US), Canada (CA), Great Britain (GB), India (IN), Germany (DE), France (FR), Japan (JP), South Korea (KR), Mexico (MX), Russia (RU), or "All Countries" global view.
- **Category Filter:** Filter all charts and metrics to a specific YouTube category (e.g., Music, Entertainment, Gaming, Science & Technology).
- **Date Range Picker:** Constrain the analysis window between November 2017 and June 2018.
- **Search:** Instant search across video titles, channels, and video IDs with automatic debouncing.
- **Teacher / Presentation Mode:** One-click toggle in top navigation or sidebar to enter the curated pedagogical presentation mode.

---

## 3. Core Modules Guide

### 3.1 Executive Overview Dashboard
- **KPI Cards:** Live counts of total trending observations, unique videos, active categories, median views, and average engagement rate.
- **Dynamic Insight Cards:** Real-time data-driven statements explaining dominant categories, engagement spikes, and trending longevity.
- **Daily Trending Volume:** Interactive line chart showing trending volume over time.
- **Category Popularity Comparison:** Side-by-side visualization of Reconciled Unique Views vs Legacy Cumulative Snapshot Sum.

### 3.2 Trending Video Explorer
- Filter by Top 10, 20, 50, or custom limits.
- Sort videos by trending appearance count, views, likes, or engagement rate.
- Click any video card to open the **Video Detail Modal**: view thumbnail, day-by-day trajectory, engagement ratios, and comparison against category benchmarks.

### 3.3 Category Intelligence
- **Category Popularity Ranking:** Horizontal bar chart comparing unique-video view totals.
- **Interactive Category Comparison:** Select 2 or more categories to contrast view distributions, engagement rates, and median performance.

### 3.4 Engagement Analytics Lab
- **Scatter Plots:** Log-scaled views vs. likes and views vs. comments.
- **Metric Rate Distributions:** Interactive histograms of like rates, comment rates, and positive interaction rates.
- **Correlation Heatmap:** Pearson and Spearman correlations between views, likes, dislikes, and comments.

### 3.5 Sentiment Intelligence
- **AFINN Lexicon Valence:** Breakdown of positive, negative, neutral, and unclassified video titles.
- **Valence Distribution:** Histogram of scores from $-5$ to $+5$.
- **Keyword Contributions:** Most impactful positive and negative keywords.

### 3.6 Global Country Comparison
- Multi-country selector to compare US, GB, IN, CA, JP, KR, DE, FR, MX, and RU.
- Country radar comparison across median views, like rates, comment rates, and repeat longevity.

### 3.7 Creator Analytics
- Discover the most prolific creators on the trending page (e.g. ESPN, The Tonight Show, WWE, Netflix).
- Bubble chart contrasting creator video volume with average view reach.

### 3.8 Machine Learning & Predictive Studio
- **Trend Continuation Prediction:** Evaluate Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and Logistic Regression with interactive ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and Confusion Matrix displays.
- **Interactive Predictor:** Input current views, likes, comments, and category to calculate real-time probability of trending tomorrow.
- **K-Means Clustering & PCA:** 2D interactive projection of content clusters with silhouette and elbow plots.
- **Category Time-Series Forecasting:** 30-day ARIMA forecast with confidence intervals.
- **Trend Intelligence Score Studio:** Interactive sliders to adjust view, engagement, and velocity weights with real-time leaderboard re-ranking.

### 3.9 Teacher / Presentation Mode
- A 10-step guided academic presentation designed for faculty reviews and project defenses.
- Advances slide by slide with concise explanations, methodologies, and visual exhibits.
